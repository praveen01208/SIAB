How a visitgoes

Five steps, the same on a dripping tap as on a new heating system.

  1. 1

    You call or book

    A person answers from 7 a.m. to 9 p.m. Tell us what's wrong, send a photo if you can, and pick a two-hour slot.

  2. 2

    We look first

    The engineer finds the cause, measures and photographs it, and shows you what they've found before touching anything.

  3. 3

    A quote in writing

    Small jobs are priced on the spot; bigger ones arrive by email within a working day, itemised, with dates.

  4. 4

    The work

    Dust sheets down, water and heating off only as long as needed, old parts taken away, the room left as we found it.

  5. 5

    Tested and signed off

    Pressure and flue tests, the certificate and warranty registered in your name, and a walk round the controls.

Boiler orheat pump?

We fit both, so we can tell you straight which one suits your house.

Keep a boiler

If the house leaks heat faster than it holds it

  • Solid walls, single glazing or a loft you can't insulate yet.
  • Small radiators you'd rather not change.
  • Nowhere outside for a unit the size of a washing machine.
Switch to a heat pump

If the house is snug and you want gas gone

  • Cavity walls filled, a loft with a thick blanket of insulation.
  • Room for a hot water cylinder and a unit outside.
  • You qualify for the £7,500 grant; we check and apply for you.

Beforeyou ask

Anything else, call 0114 000 0000 and a person will answer.

Do you charge for quotes?

No. Surveys and written quotes are free anywhere inside the ring road and £45 beyond it, taken off the bill if you go ahead.

Is everyone Gas Safe registered?

Every engineer who works on gas is, and their number is on the text we send the evening before. Ask to see the card at the door; they expect it.

How long does a new boiler take?

A like-for-like combi swap is one day. Moving it or converting from a system boiler is two. You're never left without hot water overnight.

Do you work weekends?

Saturdays for booked work, and the emergency line runs every hour of every day at the same rate.

Can you sort the heat pump grant?

Yes. We're an accredited installer, so we check you qualify, apply for you and take the grant off the price before you pay.

What guarantee do I get?

Two years on our workmanship for everything, and up to ten on boilers through the manufacturer, registered in your name on the day.
